Photos are grouped into series: a pause of more than 10 seconds between two shots starts a new one. A change applies to the photo and the following ones of its series. Changing a photo that isn't the first of its series splits the series just before it. Series are worked out over all photos, picked or not, so a photo you pick later lands in its series with that series' settings.
